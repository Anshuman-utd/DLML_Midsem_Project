## Problem statement — rough draft

Traffic congestion changes over time and spreads across connected roads, making future traffic conditions difficult to predict accurately.  
The project aims to forecast traffic conditions and, in a later phase, investigate network-aware models and simulated control strategies for managing traffic flow.

## Initial objectives

- Understand the traffic forecasting problem and document the research requirements.
- Assess suitable datasets before selecting one.
- Plan traditional ML baselines and a fair chronological evaluation for Phase 1.
- Preserve future scope for Graph Attention Networks and Lighthill–Whitham–Richards (LWR) traffic-flow models, subject to clarification and approval.

## Current status

Initial documentation only. Dataset, model choices, methodology, and member assignments are not finalized. No implementation, training, or experiments have been conducted. The owner authorized pushing this initial repository scaffold only. Further publication requires direction.
